The Quiet Architecture of Reading

A reading surface should disappear. It should hold the text at a comfortable measure, soften the contrast, and leave enough air that the eye can rest.

Good typography is invisible until it is missing.

These notes collect the small decisions behind Lucid's Markdown viewer — the same warm surface, hairline border, and quiet type used by the code blocks. The frontmatter becomes part of the document instead of a debug dump.

On warm surfaces

Pure white is bright; pure black is loud. A small amount of warmth makes long reading easier, especially when the page is lit from behind. Metadata should follow the same rule.
